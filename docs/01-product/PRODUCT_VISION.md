# Product Vision Document

> **"A modern digital ecosystem for managing, nurturing, and empowering every journey of a Qur'anic student."**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Taruna Juara Digital Platform represents the digital transformation of **Rumah Tahfidz Taruna Juara**. Rather than adopting generic ERP administrative interfaces, this platform presents a unified, experience-driven digital environment for every stakeholder:
- Prospective Students (*Calon Mahasantri*)
- Active Students (*Mahasantri*)
- Mentors & Instructors (*Ustadz / Musyrif*)
- Alumni (*Alumni*)
- Institutional Leadership (*Pengelola Lembaga*)

---

## 2. Core Strategic Pillars

### Pillar 1: Experience-Driven Interfaces
- Zero friction for daily setoran, murajaah logging, and event participation.
- Visual progress indicators (radial charts, heatmaps) that provide immediate clarity.

### Pillar 2: Nurturing Over Administrative Tracking
- Proactive identification of students needing extra support (*Early Warning System*).
- Gamified milestones celebrating 1 Juz, 5 Juz, 10 Juz, and 30 Juz completion.

### Pillar 3: Single Source of Truth via Hybrid Architecture
- Unified account lifecycle across all stages of a student's journey.
- Enterprise backend capability powered by Odoo paired with a headless React UI.

---

## 3. Product Roadmap Overview

- **Phase 1**: Public Branding & Online PMB Admissions
- **Phase 2**: Mahasantri Portal & Hafalan Progress Tracking
- **Phase 3**: Ustadz Nurturing Center & Halaqah Analytics
- **Phase 4**: Alumni Tracer Study & Post-Graduation Murajaah Hub
- **Phase 5**: Advanced Odoo Institutional Analytics & Automation
